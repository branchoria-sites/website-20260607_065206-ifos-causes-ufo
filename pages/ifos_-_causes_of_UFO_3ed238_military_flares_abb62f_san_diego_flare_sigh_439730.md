## Introduction

San Diego has produced several widely shared “UFO” episodes that were later linked to military flares. These incidents are useful examples of how unidentified flying object (IFO) reports develop in real time. Bright lights appear offshore at night, witnesses record [short clips]({{ 'short-clips/' | relative_url }}) with little depth information, social media fills the gap with speculation, and official explanations often arrive hours or days later. By that point, many viewers have already formed their own conclusions.

The San Diego cases are particularly instructive because they occurred in a region surrounded by major military facilities, naval operations and training areas. Yet even there, uncertainty about who was conducting an exercise, and when officials would confirm it, helped ordinary flare sightings evolve into viral UFO stories. <span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://abcnews.com/US/strange-lights-off-san-diego-coast-locals-taking/story?id=85849918" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: abcnews.com">[ABC News]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">abcnews.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">ABC NewsStrange lights off San Diego coast have locals taking to...June 28, 2022 — 28 Jun 2022 — Strange lights off San Diego coast have...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: June 28, 2022</span></span></span>

## The 2018 offshore flare reports

One of the clearest examples occurred in August 2018, when residents across San Diego County reported unusual lights over the Pacific. Witnesses described bright stationary lights visible from multiple locations, and photographs quickly circulated online. To many observers, the lights appeared too bright and too still to be conventional aircraft. <span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://timesofsandiego.com/military/2018/08/30/mysterious-lights-seen-over-san-diego-were-flares-from-navy-exercise/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: timesofsandiego.com">[Times of San Diego]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">timesofsandiego.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">mysterious lights seen over san diego were flares from navy exercise</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Times of San DiegoMysterious Lights Seen Over San Diego Were Flares from...30 Aug 2018 — Mysterious lights in the sky over San Diego tur...</span></span></span>

The explanation emerged relatively quickly. A Navy spokeswoman confirmed that [pilots]({{ 'pilots/' | relative_url }}) were conducting a military training exercise roughly 30 miles offshore and that the lights were training flares. Reports noted that the lights could be visible over a wide area because of their intensity and the clear coastal viewing conditions. <span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://timesofsandiego.com/military/2018/08/30/mysterious-lights-seen-over-san-diego-were-flares-from-navy-exercise/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: timesofsandiego.com">[Times of San Diego]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">timesofsandiego.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">mysterious lights seen over san diego were flares from navy exercise</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Times of San DiegoMysterious Lights Seen Over San Diego Were Flares from...30 Aug 2018 — Mysterious lights in the sky over San Diego tur...</span></span></span>

What made the event noteworthy was not merely that flares were involved, but how closely the witness descriptions matched common UFO narratives:

<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* The lights appeared to hover.
* They remained visible for an extended period.
* They seemed disconnected from any visible aircraft.
* Their position over dark ocean water made distance difficult to judge.

</div>

Offshore observers have few visual reference points, making slow descent difficult to perceive. <span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://timesofsandiego.com/military/2018/08/30/mysterious-lights-seen-over-san-diego-were-flares-from-navy-exercise/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: timesofsandiego.com">[Times of San Diego]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">timesofsandiego.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">mysterious lights seen over san diego were flares from navy exercise</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Times of San DiegoMysterious Lights Seen Over San Diego Were Flares from...30 Aug 2018 — Mysterious lights in the sky over San Diego tur...</span></span></span>

## Why official timing shapes belief

The San Diego flare episodes reveal an important pattern in UFO reporting: the speed of speculation often exceeds the speed of verification.

In both the 2018 and 2022 incidents, witnesses observed genuine lights. The key question was not whether something unusual-looking was present, but what it actually was. When official confirmation arrived quickly, as in the 2018 case, speculation remained relatively limited. When information was fragmented or delayed, as in 2022, alternative explanations flourished. <span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://timesofsandiego.com/military/2018/08/30/mysterious-lights-seen-over-san-diego-were-flares-from-navy-exercise/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: timesofsandiego.com">[Times of San Diego]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">timesofsandiego.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">mysterious lights seen over san diego were flares from navy exercise</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Times of San DiegoMysterious Lights Seen Over San Diego Were Flares from...30 Aug 2018 — Mysterious lights in the sky over San Diego tur...</span></span></span> <span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.cbs8.com/article/news/mystery-solved-military-flare-training-lights-up-the-san-diego-night-sky/509-b8fa3172-b52a-4951-8e66-354890462063" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cbs8.com">[2CBS 8]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cbs8.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The flare lights were from...Read more...</span></span></span>

Several factors amplify this effect:

**Short video clips remove context.** A thirty-second recording rarely shows how lights appeared, moved or faded over a longer period.

**Offshore locations hide scale.** A flare tens of miles away can appear close and stationary.

**Conflicting statements increase suspicion.** When one authority suggests an explanation while another says it has no information, many observers interpret the disagreement as evidence of secrecy rather than ordinary communication delays. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.facebook.com/HunterSowards13/posts/weird-lights-who-else-saw-a-strange-light-formation-in-san-diego-county-this-eve/463735838894682/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: facebook.com">[Facebook]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">facebook.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">WEIRD LIGHTS? **Online discussion rewards mystery.** A post asking “What is this?” spreads faster than a later correction identifying a routine exercise.

These factors do not require anyone to be irrational. They arise naturally from the way information travels during unexpected events.
